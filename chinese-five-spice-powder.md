> A warm and aromatic spice blend built around star anise — toast, cool, grind, and store for months of flavor.

## Table of Contents

## Ingredients (x4 — large batch)

| Spice               | Quantity                |
|---------------------|-------------------------|
| Star anise          | 16 stars (~32g)         |
| Cinnamon (sticks)   | 4 medium sticks (~20g)  |
| Cloves              | 24 to 32 cloves (~12g)  |
| Sichuan peppercorns | 4 tbsp (~20g)           |
| Fennel seeds        | 4 tbsp (~20g)           |

## Instructions

1. Toast all spices **dry** in a pan over medium heat for a few minutes until fragrant
2. Let cool completely
3. Grind finely

## Storage

Airtight jar, away from light.
Best consumed within **2-3 months** to preserve the freshness of the aromas.

## Notes

- Star anise is the dominant spice in the blend
- Chinese cinnamon (cassia) is more authentic than Ceylon cinnamon
- Toasting is essential: it releases the essential oils and rounds out the flavors
